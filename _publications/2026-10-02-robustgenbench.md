RobustGenBench is a benchmark for robust generalization to unforeseen adversarial and common perturbations, covering vision models and vision-enabled large language models. It provides six classification datasets with fixed splits and a shared evaluation protocol, and releases pre-computed adversarial images so that models can be evaluated without a GPU, including models available only through an API.

Accepted at **NeurIPS 2026, Evaluations and Datasets Track**. Poster: Thursday, December 10, 2026, Sydney — [NeurIPS page](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/loc/sydney/poster/139536).
