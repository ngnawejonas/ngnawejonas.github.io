Grokking occurs when a model memorizes training data long before it begins to generalize. This work characterizes a shell–core geometry of the solution space induced by Adam with weight-shrinkage regularization. Using stopping-time theory to study the transition from memorization to generalization, we derive scaling laws for learning rate, batch size, and regularization strength, and validate them experimentally.
